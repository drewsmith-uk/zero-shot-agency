# Day 186: Authorise the Wider Campaign Before Buying AI Overview Exposure

**Decision requested:** may this budget fund a broader campaign, or must it buy ads exclusively inside Google's AI Overviews?

Settle that before approving the spend. Google says advertisers cannot directly target placements within AI Overviews, cannot opt out of serving there, and currently receive no segmented reporting for ads shown within them.[1] An exclusive placement brief therefore asks for controls the platform does not offer.

The alternative is not automatically to cancel paid search. It is to decide whether the business wants the broader campaign on its own commercial terms.

This memo is ZSA's analysis of Google's documentation, checked on 22 October 2026—not a campaign test or an account-performance report.

<!-- more -->

## Define what the approval would buy

The scope here is ads **within** an AI Overview, not ads above or below it. Google documents text and Shopping ads from existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns as eligible for that within-Overview surface.[1]

Serving remains conditional: Google considers commercial intent, relevance to the query and the Overview's content, and the ability to win the auction.[1] Eligibility cannot become a promise that the funded campaign will appear there.

Use the following grid before signing the media brief. The controls are Google's; the approval tests are my proposed commercial reading.

| Clause to authorise | Documented position | Decision for the budget owner |
|---|---|---|
| Allowed surface | No direct AI Overview placement targeting and no AI Overview opt-out.[1] | Accept the proposed campaign's wider distribution, or refuse an exclusive-placement mandate. A requirement to exclude this surface also needs reconsideration. |
| Market and vertical | Within-Overview availability is limited by country, language and vertical.[1] | Establish eligibility for the intended audience and offer before assigning this exposure any value. |
| Targeting choice | Google requires AI-powered targeting such as broad match on Search or supported keywordless routes.[1] | Assess whether the proposed targeting is acceptable for the wider campaign, not merely whether it unlocks potential eligibility. |
| Acceptance condition | No segmented reporting for ads within AI Overviews.[1] | Agree campaign-level commercial goals; do not make approval depend on an isolated AI Overview result the documented reporting cannot supply. |

These conditions work together. Changing a reporting label cannot create placement control. Accepting wider distribution does not establish market eligibility. Satisfying eligibility does not settle whether the targeting or economics suit the business.

## Apply the same controls to two spending briefs

**Brief A: fund only ads delivered inside AI Overviews, then judge that spend through isolated AI Overview results.**

Decline this brief as written. It fails both the placement-only condition and the separate-reporting condition. Improving creative or selecting an eligible targeting route would not repair those contractual requirements. The requested purchase remains unavailable under the documented controls.

**Brief B: fund a broader eligible campaign against agreed commercial goals, accepting that ads may also serve inside AI Overviews.**

This brief can proceed to commercial assessment. What changed is the authorised distribution and acceptance condition—not Google's system. The buyer now permits wider campaign exposure and evaluates that campaign rather than demanding a separately purchased AI slot.

Proceed only if that wider campaign deserves the investment. Ask whether its audience, targeting and expected economics justify spending without a guaranteed AI Overview appearance. If the business case collapses without that guarantee, rescope or decline rather than quietly relabel Brief A.

## Clear the market check before the signature

As checked, Google's within-Overview country list does not include the UK; it describes English-language availability in specified countries on mobile and desktop.[1] That is not a statement that UK advertisers cannot use Google Ads, nor that ads above or below an Overview share the same restriction. Google documents broader availability for those positions.[1]

Google also excludes sensitive verticals from within-Overview ads, naming finance and healthcare among others.[1] Broader campaign authorisation cannot override those conditions. Recheck the current rules when assessing the proposal.

Keep organic inclusion outside this purchase. Search Central describes indexed, snippet-eligible pages as candidates for supporting links, without guaranteeing serving.[2] Neither spending brief should promise an organic supporting-link position in return for ad spend.

**Approve the wider scope deliberately, return the brief for rescoping, or decline.** Do not sign an exclusive AI Overview purchase and leave the media team to discover that the business actually authorised something else.
